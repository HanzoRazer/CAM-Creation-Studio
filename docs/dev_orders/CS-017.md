## Bounded case

One `OperationPlan`, one `ContourDefinition`, and its real tool and material
binding. The selection is exactly four connected axis-aligned `Line2D` edges
forming a nondegenerate closed rectangle in XY at Z0. Relation is `on`,
direction is `unspecified`, and stock allowance is zero. Explicit climb or
conventional requests are refused.

Units are millimetres. The frame is `stock-fixed-coupon-xy`. The tool is a
flat end mill in a fixed orientation. The profile is `synthetic-router-mm`.
Depth levels are explicit, positive, and strictly increasing, end at the
definition target depth, and respect the declared maximum removal and
stepdown. Cut and plunge feeds are explicit and positive. Stored feed advice
is not selection.

The expansion normalizes the rectangle to `(xmin, ymin)` and traverses
`(xmax, ymin) → (xmax, ymax) → (xmin, ymax) → (xmin, ymin)` as viewed from
+Z. Each depth is one plunge, four cuts, and one retract. This is ordering,
not a climb-milling claim, and it is not contour offsetting.

## Out of scope

Arbitrary polylines, holes, islands, inside or outside offsets, tabs,
ramping, helices, arcs, pocketing, drilling, cutter compensation, instrument
dimensions, controller-specific posts, and physical optimization. The frozen
importer is unchanged. No sibling repository is imported.
